NRI MD MS: Admission, Eligibility, Documents & Counselling Guide 2026
NRI MD MS admission is an important option for eligible NRI, OCI and NRI-sponsored candidates seeking postgraduate medical education in India. MD and MS programmes are pursued after MBBS, and admission is subject to NEET-PG qualification, applicable counselling rules, seat availability, and the eligibility conditions prescribed by the relevant counselling authority and institution.
Candidates considering NRI MD MS admission should carefully compare colleges, specialities, fee structures, NRI eligibility requirements, seat categories and counselling procedures before making a decision. Since rules and fees can change between counselling cycles, applicants should verify the latest information with the concerned authority.
NRI MD MS Eligibility
The basic eligibility for postgraduate medical admission generally includes a recognized MBBS qualification, completion of the required internship and qualification in NEET-PG. NRI-category candidates may additionally need to establish their NRI, OCI or other applicable status according to the rules of the particular counselling authority.
For NRI-sponsored candidates, documentation establishing the relationship with the eligible NRI sponsor may also be required. The exact relationship and documentation requirements can vary according to the applicable rules and state or institutional counselling process.
Documents Required for NRI MD MS Admission
Candidates should prepare their documents well before counselling. Depending on the applicable NRI rules, documents can include:
NEET-PG admit card and scorecard
MBBS degree and academic records
Internship completion certificate
Valid passport and visa/residence documents
NRI, OCI or applicable status proof
NRI certificate issued by the appropriate authority, where required
Relationship certificate for NRI-sponsored candidates
Sponsorship affidavit, where applicable
NRE/NRO bank account or financial-support documentation
Class 10 and Class 12 certificates
Birth certificate
Other documents prescribed by the counselling authority

NRI MD MS Fees and College Selection
Fees for NRI-category MD/MS seats can vary substantially depending on the medical college, deemed university, speciality, state and applicable fee notification. Therefore, candidates should not rely on an old fee list when planning their admission budget.

Candidates should compare the complete cost of the course rather than looking only at tuition fees. Additional expenses can include hostel charges, university fees, examination charges, deposits and other institutional expenses.
NRI MD MS Counselling Process
The admission process normally requires candidates to follow the applicable NEET-PG counselling procedure. A typical process involves:
Qualifying NEET-PG.
Checking NRI eligibility and documentation requirements.
Registering for the applicable counselling.
Completing NRI-category verification wherever required.
Reviewing eligible MD/MS colleges and branches.
Filling and locking choices.
Participating in seat allotment rounds.
Verifying the allotted seat and fee structure.
Completing document verification and college reporting.
The exact process can differ between MCC, state counselling authorities and individual institutional rules, so candidates should check the current official notification before submitting choices.
Choosing the Right MD/MS Branch
Candidates should evaluate their NEET-PG rank, preferred speciality, budget, college location, hospital exposure, available seats and future career plans. Common MD/MS options include General Medicine, Pediatrics, Radiodiagnosis, Dermatology, Anaesthesiology, Psychiatry, General Surgery, Orthopaedics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology and other recognised postgraduate specialities.
Previous cut-offs can be useful for planning, but they should be treated only as a reference because seat availability, candidate choices, counselling rounds and rules can change each year.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is NEET-PG required for NRI MD MS admission?
 NEET-PG qualification is an important requirement for postgraduate medical admission, subject to the applicable rules and category requirements.
Can an NRI sponsor a candidate for MD/MS admission?
 NRI-sponsored admission may be available where permitted by the applicable counselling rules. Candidates must provide the required sponsorship and relationship documents.
Are NRI MD/MS fees the same in every college?
 No. Fees can vary according to the college, speciality, state, seat category and current fee notification.
Where can I check NRI MD/MS admission information?
 Candidates should check the latest official counselling notification and the concerned medical college's current fee and admission information before making any payment or choice.
